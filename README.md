## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **METAL GEAR SOLID 4: Guns of the Patriots - Master Collection Version**. This trainer provides optional single-player enhancements for Snake's core systems, including Life, Psyche, ammunition, weapon handling, camouflage, equipment batteries, Drebin Points, and alert management. ## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into active gameplay rather than on the title screen. Movement, crouching, crawling, wall pressing, hanging, rolling, and other traversal actions remain governed by the game's normal controls. Enhanced camouflage is most effective when Snake uses low stances, stays still, or presses against environmental surfaces.

### Tools & Inventory

Drebin Points can be used to buy weapons and ammunition, wash ID-locked weapons, and apply available weapon customizations. Unlimited ammunition affects compatible ammunition counts but does not automatically equip weapons or items. Open the pause menu to manage weapons, equipment, camouflage, Solid Eye, and Metal Gear Mk. II normally.

### World & Environment

The trainer is intended for the game's single-player campaign and its mission areas. No changes are made to objectives, cutscenes, briefings, or the story sequence. Features affecting alerts, timers, or camouflage may alter intended stealth challenges and should be disabled before scripted events if unusual behavior occurs.

### Combat & Weapons

Infinite Life and Psyche reduce the consequences of enemy fire, stress, and battlefield conditions. Infinite ammunition and No Reload support firearms and other compatible weapons, while normal weapon behavior, aiming, CQC actions, suppressors, tranquilizers, explosives, and enemy reactions remain part of the game's combat system.
